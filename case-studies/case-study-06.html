Introduction

In Fall 2025 (DWDD 2610 with Paul Cheney) I learned SCSS, a superset of CSS that allows nesting and variables. SCSS lets you reuse colors via $brand-color values and nest styles for cleaner structure, making broad theme changes easier.

What happened

Throughout the semester I layered SCSS into my sites as a major upgrade from vanilla CSS. I reused components across projects, changing themes quickly by updating $brand-color variables. SCSS helpers like color opacity functions made overlays and icon animations easier to control.

Reusing previous site elements while swapping brand values saved time and kept designs consistent with new themes as assignments progressed.

Conclusion

SCSS enabled more intricate, well-structured, and visually consistent sites. The organizational benefits felt closer to HTML’s clarity than traditional CSS.
